# Pricing Strategy

Develop or audit a pricing strategy for `$ARGUMENTS` (product, service, or company).

## Frameworks Applied

- **Value-based pricing**: willingness to pay, customer value drivers
- **Competitive pricing**: benchmark vs. top 3 competitors
- **Cost-plus pricing**: floor price from unit economics
- **Price architecture**: tiers, bundles, freemium, usage-based

## Analysis Steps

1. **Understand the product**: what value does it deliver, to whom?
2. **Customer segments**: who pays most, who is price-sensitive?
3. **Competitive benchmarks**: what do alternatives cost?
4. **Price floor**: COGS + minimum margin
5. **Price ceiling**: max WTP from customer value
6. **Recommended model**: choose the best pricing mechanism with rationale
7. **Tier design** (if applicable): Free / Pro / Enterprise with feature gates
8. **Price point recommendation**: specific number(s) with confidence range

## Output

- Pricing model recommendation with justification
- Tier table (if tiered)
- Key risks (churn triggers, price anchoring issues)
- A/B test suggestions to validate

Use `$ARGUMENTS` to specify context: `[product name] [target market] [current price if any]`
