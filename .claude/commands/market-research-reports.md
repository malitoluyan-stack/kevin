# Market Research Reports

Generate a structured market research report on `$ARGUMENTS` (industry, product, or company).

## Report Structure

1. **Executive Summary** — market size, growth rate, key opportunity in 3 bullets
2. **Market Overview** — definition, scope, segmentation
3. **Market Size & Growth** — TAM / SAM / SOM, CAGR, 5-year forecast
4. **Competitive Landscape** — top 5 players, market share, positioning map description
5. **Customer Analysis** — target segments, pain points, buying behavior
6. **Trend Analysis** — 3–5 macro trends shaping the market
7. **Opportunity Assessment** — whitespace, underserved segments
8. **Risks & Barriers** — regulatory, competitive, technical
9. **Recommendations** — 3 actionable strategic recommendations

## Behavior

- Use WebSearch/WebFetch to gather real data if search tools are available
- Cite sources for any statistics or market figures
- If data is unavailable, clearly label estimates as such with reasoning
- Keep total report under 1,500 words unless asked for depth

## Output

Use the structure above with clear headers. End with a one-page executive summary suitable for C-suite presentation.
